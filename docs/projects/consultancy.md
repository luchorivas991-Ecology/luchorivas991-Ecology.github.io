# Ecological Consultancy

Applied ecological consultancy projects integrating biodiversity science, spatial analysis, remote sensing, statistical modelling, and artificial intelligence.

The projects presented in this section are independent consultancy case studies developed using real-world environmental data and professional analytical workflows.

They are designed around realistic client scenarios and environmental decision making problems, with an emphasis on reproducibility, scientific transparency, and practical conservation outcomes.

---

## Areas of Expertise

- Biodiversity impact assessment
- Biodiversity credits and nature markets
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)
- Renewable energy and biodiversity
- Mining, oil and gas environmental assessment
- Species Distribution Modelling
- Ecological connectivity
- Remote sensing
- Biodiversity monitoring
- Artificial intelligence for species detection
- Spatial biodiversity risk assessment
- Conservation planning
